## 1. Purpose, definitions, and scope

This Agreement governs material submitted by Contributor for possible inclusion in OpenAMRobot, including source and object code, firmware, inventions, hardware and CAD designs, schematics, documentation, diagrams, tests, databases, datasets, models, media, specifications, and other protectable material (“Contribution”).

“Submit” means intentionally providing a Contribution to an official OpenAMRobot repository, pull request, issue, review, mailing channel, or another contribution channel designated by the Company.

“Accepted Contribution” means a Contribution merged by the Company or an authorized maintainer into an official OpenAMRobot repository, or expressly accepted by the Company in writing.

This Agreement applies only to Contributor’s rights in Accepted Contributions. It does not transfer Contributor’s pre-existing material clearly identified in writing before acceptance (“Background Material”), except for the licence in section 3 necessary to use an Accepted Contribution.

3. rights in contribution-specific technical inventions, improvements, discoveries, and know-how embodied in the Accepted Contribution, including the right to apply for, prosecute, maintain, enforce, license, and assign patents and registered designs; and
4. all rights to claim and retain damages or other remedies for infringement occurring after the assignment,

in each case only to the extent owned or controlled by Contributor, for the full term of the relevant rights and all renewals, revivals, and extensions.

The assignment includes rights to reproduce, modify, adapt, translate, combine, distribute, publish, communicate, display, perform, manufacture, have manufactured, use, sell, offer for sale, import, commercialize, license, sublicense, enforce, and create derivative works.

To the extent a right cannot validly be assigned automatically, Contributor:

- holds that right on trust for the Company where legally permitted;
- grants the Company an exclusive, irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide, fully paid, royalty-free, transferable, sublicensable licence to exercise it for any purpose; and
- will execute reasonable confirmatory documents under section 9.

If exclusivity or a trust is not legally permitted, the licence is non-exclusive only to the minimum extent required by mandatory law and otherwise has the same scope.

Nothing assigns identified third-party material. Contributor grants only rights Contributor is legally entitled to grant.
