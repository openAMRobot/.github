# AI-Assisted Contribution Policy

AI tools may assist OpenAMRobot work, but the human contributor remains responsible for every submitted line, design, statement, and asset.

A contributor must:

- disclose material generative-AI assistance in the pull request;
- review output for correctness, safety, security, licence compatibility, provenance, and substantial similarity;
- confirm authority to submit all prompts, inputs, references, and outputs;
- avoid entering confidential information, personal data, credentials, client material, unpublished inventions, or restricted source into an AI service;
- identify third-party material reproduced or adapted through the tool;
- test generated code and validate generated engineering content.

AI output with unclear provenance, incompatible terms, invented attribution, or unverifiable technical claims must not be accepted.

Use of an AI tool does not change DCO or Contributor Agreement requirements and does not shift responsibility from the contributor or reviewer.
