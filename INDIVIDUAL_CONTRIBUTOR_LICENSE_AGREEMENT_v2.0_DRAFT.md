This Contributor License Agreement (“Agreement”) is proposed between **Botshare LTD**, Company ID HE479056, Limassol, Cyprus (“Company”), and the individual who affirmatively accepts it (“Contributor”).

## 1. Scope and acceptance

A “Contribution” is original code, firmware, documentation, CAD, hardware design, schemas, databases, datasets, models, or other protectable material intentionally submitted for possible inclusion in an official OpenAMRobot project. An “Accepted Contribution” is a Contribution merged by an authorized maintainer or expressly accepted in writing by Company. This Agreement covers only rights Contributor owns or has authority to license. Submission alone does not require acceptance.

The version affirmatively accepted by Contributor controls its covered Contributions. This proposed document has no effect on previous assignments or accepted agreements.

## 2. Contributor retains ownership; broad non-exclusive grant

**Contributor retains all ownership of copyright and other intellectual property rights in Contributor's own Contributions. Nothing in this Agreement assigns or transfers ownership to Company.** Contributor remains free to use, modify, distribute, license, and commercialize Contributor's own work independently, including in other projects, subject to existing third-party rights and contracts. Contributor does not acquire rights in other contributors' material or Company material merely by contributing.

For each Accepted Contribution, Contributor grants Company a **non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, fully paid, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable licence**, to the maximum extent permitted by law, under all copyright and related rights Contributor can license, to use, reproduce, modify, adapt, translate, combine, distribute, publish, publicly display and perform, make derivative works, make available, maintain, manufacture and have manufactured, use, sell, offer for sale, import, commercialize, license, sublicense and otherwise exploit that Accepted Contribution **for any purpose**, including commercial and proprietary products and services, with or without modification. The grant permits Company to transfer or sublicense rights to customers, distributors, contractors, affiliates, successors and acquirers, and to license derivative and combined works, without obtaining new consent or paying royalties.

This grant survives withdrawal from the project, account deletion, and termination of this Agreement. It applies only to rights lawfully controlled by Contributor; it does not remove attribution or notice requirements imposed by mandatory law or valid upstream licences.

## 3. Public outbound licence and project consistency

Contributor authorizes Company to distribute Accepted Contributions as part of OpenAMRobot under the asset-appropriate published project licence:
- Original software and firmware: **MIT**.
- Original hardware source, CAD, PCB, schematics, manufacturing sources and CAD-derived robot geometry: **CERN-OHL-P-2.0**.
- Original documentation, diagrams, tutorials, images and educational content: **CC BY 4.0**.
- Datasets and AI models: **an explicit asset-specific licence approved before publication; no licence by implication**.
- Logos, trademarks and brand assets: **excluded unless expressly licensed**.

Company may also license, sublicense or commercialize material under other terms, including proprietary or dual licences, **only to the extent rights granted here and other applicable rights permit**. Company may not purport to relicense third-party material without authority.

Contributor agrees not to attach additional restrictions, incompatible copyleft terms, conflicting licence notices or contributor-specific approval requirements to original Accepted Contributions without explicit written approval before acceptance. This agreement does not automatically make third-party material MIT-compatible or remove required notices.

## 4. Background material, third parties and permissions

Contributor retains ownership of identifiable pre-existing “Background Material” and identifies it, including upstream URL, rights holder, licence, version, copied files and relevant restrictions, in the PR before acceptance. Ownership of Background Material and third-party material is never assigned.

If an Accepted Contribution incorporates Contributor-owned Background Material, Contributor grants Company and downstream recipients the non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free rights needed to use that material in, with, and as necessary to exploit the Accepted Contribution, **but only to the extent Contributor can lawfully grant those rights and subject to disclosed restrictions**. This does not silently override an incompatible background licence. Company may reject, remove or separately negotiate any material whose rights do not support the intended outbound licence.

Contributor must not present Apache-2.0, GPL, non-commercial, proprietary, confidential, employer-owned, university-owned or unlicensed material as original MIT-licensed material. All third-party components require provenance and a documented compatibility determination. Open-source software dependencies can remain independently licensed when explicitly approved and tracked; this is not automatic relicensing.

## 5. Patents and other technical rights

Contributor retains ownership of patents and inventions. Contributor grants Company and recipients of each Accepted Contribution a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable (except defensive termination below), royalty-free, transferable, sublicensable licence under patent claims Contributor can license that are necessarily infringed by making, having made, using, selling, offering for sale, importing or distributing the Accepted Contribution as submitted, alone or in the OpenAMRobot combination submitted. Unrelated claims and later unrelated combinations are not licensed.

A recipient's patent licence may terminate if that recipient initiates patent litigation alleging the Accepted Contribution or OpenAMRobot infringes that recipient's patent, except defensive counterclaims and subject to mandatory law. This Agreement is not an assignment of inventions or patent title. Separate written agreements may be needed for specific jointly developed inventions or registered rights.

## 6. Authorship, moral rights and attribution

Contributor remains the author/inventor of Contributor's original work and retains any non-transferable moral rights. Company will preserve accurate attribution in Git history or comparable project records where reasonably practicable and comply with mandatory attribution and applicable licence notices.

To the extent permitted by law, Contributor consents to Company's good-faith modifications, combinations, translations, publication or removal of Accepted Contributions and agrees not to assert waivable moral rights solely to prevent uses licensed by this Agreement. **This is not a blanket waiver of authorship or a transfer of moral rights.** Company may not falsely present another person as the original author. Attribution in every interface or shipped unit is not promised unless required by law or licence.

## 7. Contributor representations

Contributor confirms that: (a) Contributor has authority to grant these rights; (b) all employer, client, sponsor, co-author, inventor and institutional approvals needed have been obtained; (c) third-party, Background and materially AI-assisted content is disclosed with provenance and licences; (d) the submission contains no unauthorized confidential or personal information, trade secrets or credentials; (e) Contribution is not knowingly infringing; (f) asset-specific outbound terms, DCO and project security/contribution rules are followed. If authority is organizational, use a suitable approved corporate agreement rather than accepting this as an unauthorized individual.

## 8. DCO, records and acceptance

Every commit requires a valid DCO sign-off. DCO certifies provenance and authority; it does not assign IP and does not replace this Agreement. Company may retain records of agreement version, electronic acceptance, verified GitHub identity, pull requests, commit hashes and approval. A green automated check cannot cure deficient authority or third-party licensing.

## 9. No obligation or relationship; no warranty

Company has no obligation to merge, use, publish or compensate for a Contribution. Unless separately agreed, neither party owes royalties or fees. No employment, partnership, joint venture or authority to bind Company arises. Contributions are supplied without warranty except express authority and provenance representations, and neither party excludes liability that mandatory law prohibits excluding.

## 10. Term, survival and updates

Either party may terminate this Agreement prospectively by written notice. Existing grants for previously Accepted Contributions survive and remain irrevocable to the extent legally permitted. Termination does not retroactively undo a contribution's public open-source licence. A material amendment requires affirmative acceptance of a new version; publication of changed Markdown or modification of a CLA Assistant URL alone cannot amend an accepted agreement. Existing ICLA v1.1 assignments are **not** retroactively reversed.

Botshare LTD processes personal data under the Contributor Privacy Notice. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Cyprus, subject to mandatory protections; Cyprus courts have jurisdiction subject to non-waivable rules. Electronic acceptance must create a reliable, retrievable record. A court's invalidation of a provision does not invalidate other enforceable provisions.
