# Contributor agreement transition to version 2.0

Status: version 2.0 was activated on 22 November 2026, 18:05 UTC, after legal review and owner approval. The live status and the activation evidence are in [CLA.md](CLA.md).

## What changes

Version 1.1 assigns transferable economic rights in accepted contributions to Botshare LTD. Under version 2.0 the contributor keeps ownership and grants Botshare LTD a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable licence, with patent rights, for any purpose. The public project licences do not change.

## What does not change

- Contributions accepted under version 1.1 stay assigned. This transition reverses nothing.
- DCO sign-off on every commit.
- Outbound licences: MIT, CERN-OHL-P-2.0, CC BY 4.0.

## Before activation

1. Legal review by Cyprus-qualified intellectual property counsel of: enforceability and irrevocability of the licence, the patent licence and its termination clause, contributors under 18 and the guardian consent, and the effect on version 1.1 assignments (including whether click-through assignments were valid, and the fallback licence).
2. Written approval of the final texts by Botshare LTD's director.
3. The activation steps in [CLA.md](CLA.md), in order. No contributor is treated as covered by version 2.0 before a CLA Assistant record of that contributor's acceptance exists, dated on or after the activation date.

## At activation

1. Notify contributors: what changes, that earlier contributions stay under version 1.1, and that the next pull request asks for a new acceptance.
2. Send organizations with a signed version 1.1 corporate agreement the version 2.0 corporate agreement for signature. Each stays under version 1.1 until both parties have signed.
3. Obtain guardian consent for every contributor under 18 before merging further work from them, with the box for earlier contributions ticked where they have any.
4. Recheck every open external pull request. A pull request whose author has not accepted version 2.0 is not merged, unless Botshare LTD decides and records that it merges under version 1.1 with the author's agreement.
5. Update the contribution, licensing and notice files in the other OpenAMRobot repositories that still describe assignment only.

## After activation

- Do not switch CLA Assistant back to version 1.1. Correct a non-material error as 2.0.1; a material change is a new version that needs acceptance.
- One month later, reconcile the CLA Assistant export with merged pull requests and record any gap.

## Separate from this agreement

People who work for Botshare LTD for pay, as employees or contractors, are covered by their own written agreements with Botshare LTD, not by the contributor agreement.
