# Contributor Agreement recordkeeping

Botshare LTD must retain an auditable private record of Contributor Agreement acceptance. This public document defines the minimum operational record; signed agreements, identity data, and private evidence must not be committed to a public repository.

## Required record

For each contributor, record privately:

- legal name and verified contact email;
- GitHub username and, where applicable, employer or represented entity;
- agreement type (ICLA or CCLA), exact version, acceptance method, and acceptance timestamp;
- CCLA signatory authority and the covered contributor list;
- repository, pull request number, and accepted commit hashes;
- disclosed Background IP, third-party material, AI assistance, employer, university, client, sponsor, or co-author permissions;
- accepting maintainer and merge timestamp; and
- any exception, withdrawal, superseding agreement, or legal follow-up.

Export CLA Assistant acceptance records regularly and retain them with the immutable agreement version that the contributor accepted. Restrict access to authorized Botshare LTD personnel and apply the Contributor Privacy Notice and applicable retention requirements.

DCO status and Git history support provenance but do not replace the Contributor Agreement record.
