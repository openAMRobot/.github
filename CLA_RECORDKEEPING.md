# Contributor Agreement recordkeeping

Botshare LTD must retain an auditable private record of Contributor Agreement acceptance. This public document defines the minimum operational record; signed agreements, identity data, and private evidence must not be committed to a public repository.

## Required record

For each contributor, record privately:

- legal name and verified contact email;
- GitHub username and, where applicable, employer or represented entity;
- agreement type (ICLA or CCLA), document identifier and exact version, acceptance method, and acceptance timestamp;
- for a contributor under 18, that guardian consent is on record and its date (the form itself and the date of birth stay in the private file only);
- CCLA signatory authority and the covered contributor list;
- repository, pull request number, and accepted commit hashes;
- disclosed Background IP, third-party material, AI assistance, employer, university, client, sponsor, or co-author permissions;
- accepting maintainer and merge timestamp; and
- any exception, withdrawal, superseding agreement, or legal follow-up.

A contributor's coverage under a version starts at the recorded acceptance time of that version, never earlier. When a contributor moves from version 1.1 to version 2.0, keep both records: contributions accepted before the version 2.0 acceptance time stay under version 1.1.

Export CLA Assistant acceptance records regularly, and always before the organization-wide agreement is switched to a new version, and retain them with the immutable agreement version that the contributor accepted. Restrict access to authorized Botshare LTD personnel and apply the Contributor Privacy Notice and applicable retention requirements.

DCO status and Git history support provenance but do not replace the Contributor Agreement record.
