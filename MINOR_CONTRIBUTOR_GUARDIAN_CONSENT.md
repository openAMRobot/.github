# OpenAMRobot Guardian Consent for Contributors under 18

**Document identifier:** OpenAMRobot-GC-1.0  
**Version:** 1.0, 22 November 2026  
**Used with:** [Individual Contributor License Agreement version 2.0](INDIVIDUAL_CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md) ("Agreement")

A person under 18 may contribute to OpenAMRobot when a parent or legal guardian completes this form. Send the signed form privately to **info@botshare.ai**. Do not post it, or any personal details from it, in a pull request, issue or repository. Contributions are merged only after Botshare LTD has confirmed in writing that the form is on record.

## 1. Who signs

- "Minor": the contributor, who is under 18.
- "Guardian": a parent or other person with legal authority to act for the Minor.

## 2. What the Guardian agrees

2.1 The Guardian has read the Agreement and has explained it to the Minor.

2.2 The Guardian confirms having legal authority to act for the Minor in this matter under the law of the country where the Minor lives. Where that law requires the consent of both parents or of another person or authority, the Guardian confirms that this consent has been obtained.

2.3 The Guardian enters the Agreement on the Minor's behalf and in the Guardian's own name, and grants Botshare LTD the licences in the Agreement for the Minor's Accepted Contributions.

2.4 This consent covers Contributions of the Minor accepted from the date below. Where the box in section 5 is ticked, it also covers Contributions of the Minor that were accepted before that date, which are licensed on the terms of the Agreement from the date each was accepted.

2.5 The Minor keeps ownership of the Minor's Contributions, as the Agreement provides. No payment is owed by either side.

2.6 The Guardian consents to Botshare LTD processing the personal data on this form, and the Minor's public GitHub contribution record, as described in the [Contributor Privacy Notice](CONTRIBUTOR_PRIVACY_NOTICE.md).

2.7 The Guardian may withdraw this consent for future Contributions by writing to info@botshare.ai. Licences for Contributions accepted before the withdrawal continue, as the Agreement provides.

## 3. What the Minor confirms

The Minor has read the Agreement, understands that accepted work is licensed to Botshare LTD permanently and may be used commercially, and wishes to contribute on those terms. On reaching 18, the Minor will accept the Agreement personally at the next contribution.

## 4. What Botshare LTD does

Botshare LTD keeps this form privately, records only "guardian consent on record" with the GitHub username and the date in its contributor record, and deletes the Minor's date of birth once the Minor has accepted the Agreement personally as an adult.
