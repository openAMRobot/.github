**Agreement version:** 2.0, 22 November 2026  
**Status:** approved text. It applies to an organization only from the date both parties have signed it. Until then, a Corporate Contributor Agreement version 1.1 signed by that organization continues to apply to it.

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement") is between **Botshare LTD**, Company ID HE479056, VAT 60197356L, of Chrysanthou Mylona 1, Panayides Building, Floor 2, Office 1, 3030 Limassol, Cyprus ("Company"), and the legal entity identified in the signature section ("Contributor Organization").

In consideration of the parties' mutual promises, and of Company's evaluation, possible acceptance, maintenance, publication and distribution of Contributions, the parties agree as follows.

## 1. Incorporated terms

1.1 The [Individual Contributor License Agreement version 2.0](INDIVIDUAL_CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md), document identifier OpenAMRobot-ICLA-2.0 ("Individual Terms"), sections 1 to 9, 12, 14 and 15, form part of this Agreement, read with "Contributor Organization" in place of "Contributor" and with the changes below. Where this Agreement and the Individual Terms differ, this Agreement prevails.

1.2 "Contribution" means material Submitted by an Authorized Contributor within the authority given by Contributor Organization.

1.3 "Authorized Contributor" means an employee, officer, contractor or other representative of Contributor Organization listed in Schedule A, as changed under section 3.

## 2. Ownership and licence

2.1 Contributor Organization keeps ownership of the intellectual property rights it holds in its Contributions and remains free to use, license and commercialize them anywhere. No ownership is assigned to Company. Section 2 of the Individual Terms applies to Company's own work.

2.2 Contributor Organization grants Company, Company Group and Downstream Parties the licences in sections 3, 4, 6 and 7 of the Individual Terms for every Accepted Contribution, covering all rights that Contributor Organization owns or can license, including rights it obtained from its employees and contractors.

2.3 The patent licence covers patent claims owned or licensable by Contributor Organization and by the entities it controls.

## 3. Authorized Contributors

3.1 Contributor Organization keeps Schedule A accurate. An addition takes effect when Company acknowledges it in writing. A removal takes effect for future Contributions when Company receives the notice.

3.2 Removal of a person, a change of that person's employment, or the end of this Agreement does not affect the licences for Contributions accepted before it.

3.3 Each Authorized Contributor uses an attributable GitHub account, signs every commit under the DCO and follows the project policies. Authorized Contributors do not need to accept the Individual Terms for work covered by this Agreement.

3.4 A person who also contributes in a personal capacity, outside the authority of Contributor Organization, needs a recorded acceptance of the Individual Contributor License Agreement for that work.

3.5 Contributor Organization names a point of contact in Schedule B for notices under this Agreement.

## 4. Statements of Contributor Organization

Contributor Organization confirms, at each submission and acceptance, that:

(a) the person signing has authority to bind it;

(b) it owns or controls the rights licensed, and has obtained from its employees, contractors, co-authors and inventors all assignments, permissions and consents needed, including the consents in section 8 of the Individual Terms;

(c) the statements in sections 5, 6, 9 and 10(b), (d), (e) and (f) of the Individual Terms are true for each Contribution;

(d) it remains responsible for any employee-inventor compensation or similar payment owed under its own relationships. None is owed by Company Group or Downstream Parties.

## 5. Relation to version 1.1

5.1 If Contributor Organization signed Corporate Contributor Agreement version 1.1, this Agreement replaces it for every Contribution that becomes an Accepted Contribution after the date both parties have signed this Agreement. Company agrees that no assignment under version 1.1 takes place for those Contributions.

5.2 Contributions accepted under version 1.1 before that date remain governed by version 1.1. Assignments already made under it remain in effect and are not reversed by this Agreement.

5.3 If an assignment under version 1.1 is found not to have been effective for a Contribution, the licences in this Agreement apply to that Contribution from the date it was accepted.

## 6. Signature and records

6.1 This Agreement is signed by an authorized representative of each party, by handwritten signature on a scanned copy or by an advanced or qualified electronic signature, and sent privately to info@botshare.ai. It is not accepted through CLA Assistant.

6.2 Contributions of an organization are treated as covered by this version only after Company has confirmed in writing that the signed Agreement and Schedule A are on record.

6.3 Signed agreements and schedules are not published in any repository.
